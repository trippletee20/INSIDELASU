INSIDE LASU v0.11 — TWO-LANE SHUTTLE / DEADLOCK CORRECTION
========================================================
This release starts from v0.10, the version the user confirmed loads and plays.
No map topology, building locations, road centreline data, economy, saved profile,
interior, gate, task, vehicle-fare or ad content changes were made.

WHAT WAS WRONG
- Each road was visually one narrow strip; every shuttle travelled on the same
  centreline, independent of direction.
- A circular 8.5-metre spacing test blocked opposing vehicles even when room
  should have existed to pass. Opposing buses could wait forever.

WHAT CHANGED
- All drivable road segments are visibly TWO-WAY, usually 7.2 metres asphalt;
  two narrow clearances beside Incomplete Hostel U2 and Love Garden are 6.2m.
  Both widths are sufficient for two 2.35m-wide buses in adjacent lanes.
- Right-hand travel: each moving shuttle runs approximately 1.55 metres to the
  right of the centreline for its present direction. Oncoming buses now have
  their own separate trajectories rather than head-on approaches.
- Collision checking now uses the actual vehicle's oriented rectangular body,
  including extra safety margin, not an overly large fixed-radius circle.
- Intersections have a stable deterministic priority rule based on vehicle
  service ordering. Lower-priority buses yield before crossing the junction.
- Shuttle stop turnaround now includes a 4-second gradual lane transition
  during the scheduled stop; no instant horizontal jump on direction flip.
- A bus waiting at a route terminal reserves a safe approach space so another
  shuttle cannot box it in while it completes its lane change.
- Original route graph and building footprints remain untouched; paths still
  use the approved roads, including the Sports Centre bypass.

TEST THIS VERSION
1. Extract ZIP to a NEW folder. Godot > Import > project.godot, then F5.
2. Watch two shuttles drive TOWARD one another on a straight road.
3. Test a junction in Central Campus while other shuttles are moving.
4. Board a shuttle, use Ride & Watch, alight; also test Quick Travel.
5. Inspect both stops where buses change direction. Movement should be gradual.
6. Check that no bus cuts across the Sports Centre or through other buildings.

STATIC VALIDATION
  python tools/validate_v010_release.py
  python tools/validate_road_safety.py
  python tools/validate_v011_traffic.py
These tests verify data integrity, two-lane clearance, no overlap with
mapped protected footprints, and synthetic collision cases. They DO NOT run
Godot 4.7 or guarantee every edge-case is deadlock-free. Runtime regression
playtesting on the user's computer remains necessary.

IMPORTANT
- The turnaround uses a simplified 4-second lateral lane shift during the
  bus-stop dwell, not realistic real-world turning physics. Future passes can
  add proper end-stop turning loops where the campus geometry permits.
- The new traffic priority system is a first stable traffic prototype, not a
  full metropolitan traffic AI. A broader simulation test suite is recommended.
