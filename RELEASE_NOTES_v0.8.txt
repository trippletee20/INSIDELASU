- Supported Student: ₦200,000–₦2,000,000.
- Independent Hustler: ₦50,000–₦250,000.
- Scholarship Student: ₦50,000–₦300,000.
- Daily reward: ₦5,000–₦2,000,000; every player can claim once per local calendar date; high rewards are rare.
- Shuttle fare: ₦150 per ride.
- Hostel rent: ₦525,000 per full academic session. First session prepaid; future renewal system is planned but not yet activated.
