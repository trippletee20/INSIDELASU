How booking will work

  1. Choose a billboard and campaign dates.
  2. Upload your artwork for review.
  3. After approval, pay the confirmed price through Paystack or Flutterwave.
  4. Your ad appears in the game for the booked period after payment is verified.

Campaigns will be labelled as advertising. Payment status and publication will be handled by the advertising service; the game wallet is separate.
