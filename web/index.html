Before you play

This is an early playable beta. Buildings and interiors are stylised, activities are simplified, and NPC campus life is still being developed. It is not an official university service.

Your progress stays in this browser on this device. There is no account or cloud save. Clearing site data or using private browsing can remove it. All money is fictional; there are no real-money purchases.

Controls & help

WASD move · Mouse look · Shift run · Space jump · E interact · F sit/stand · M map · P profile · B wallet · H help · Esc release mouse or close a menu.

Click the game to capture the mouse; press Esc to release it. If the game cannot start, enable hardware acceleration and WebGL in your browser, close other heavy tabs and reload. A keyboard and mouse are required; this beta does not have touch controls.
