Before you play

This is an early playable beta. Buildings and interiors are stylised, activities are simplified, with role-based staff NPCs and separate room destinations. It is not an official university service.

Your progress stays in this browser on this device. Game progress has no cloud save. Social accounts and messaging require the campus database setup; local gameplay works independently. Clearing site data or using private browsing can remove it. The game wallet uses fictional money. Paid billboard bookings are planned for after launch and are not open yet. Preview a billboard ad.

Controls & help

WASD move · Mouse look · Shift run · Space jump · E interact · F sit/stand · M map · P profile · B wallet · H help · T phone · V inside/outside shuttle view · Esc release mouse or close a menu.

Click the game to capture the mouse; press Esc to release it. If the game cannot start, enable hardware acceleration and WebGL in your browser, close other heavy tabs and reload. On phones: use the left stick to move, swipe on the right to look, and tap Interact near buildings or shuttles. Open Phone for notes, a planner and campus apps. Use Expand/Hide to control the HUD, and Map for the campus overview. Rotate your phone if you prefer a wider view.
