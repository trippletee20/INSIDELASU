INSIDE LASU v0.8.2 — CHANGELOG

1. Shuttle road routing
   - Both regular service routes are rebuilt from the connected road graph.
   - Destination routes project to actual road segments and follow the graph through
     intersections. Small endpoint repairs are rejected if they intersect mapped
     building footprints.
   - Smooth physics-tick movement and gradual body turning remain enabled.

2. Destination bus stops and pickup calls
   - Every selectable campus destination receives a stop/drop-off record.
   - Compact roadside stands are created where a covered shelter or nearby stand is
     not already available; nearby destinations share a stand to prevent pole overlap.
   - Press E at a stop to dispatch the nearest available shuttle. It drives along roads,
     waits up to 45 seconds for boarding, and returns if nobody boards.
   - Existing shelter pickup points are preserved even when a shelter name differs from
     a building destination name.

3. Travel choice
   - Ride & Watch follows the complete route and alights at the designated stop.
   - Quick Travel charges the same ₦150 fare, skips the viewing animation, places the
     player at the same stand, and does not teleport the shuttle.
   - The menu displays approximate travel time and a final-walk hint.

4. Sitting
   - Bench and seat surfaces in covered shelters, gardens and the sports spectator area
     receive interaction anchors. Press E to sit and E again to stand.
   - Seat registration supports future chair/seat surfaces by naming convention or group.

5. HUD / interaction improvements
   - The player gets a call-shuttle prompt at a stand, a notice when a shuttle is already
     en route, and a waiting prompt when it arrives.
   - Shuttle boarding proximity was reduced to avoid boarding a vehicle from too far away.
   - Stop status signs can display SHUTTLE COMING and SHUTTLE HERE.

6. Layout preservation
   - campus_layout.json: unchanged.
   - road_network_v0.4.json: unchanged.
   - Student profile, wallet, daily reward, hostel, Arcade, billboards, landscaping and
     Franklas Gate assets are retained.
